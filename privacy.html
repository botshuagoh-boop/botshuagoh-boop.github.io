Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Hermes is a private assistant used only by its owner. The only Google account it connects to is its own (botshuagoh@gmail.com), plus calendars its owner has chosen to share with that account.

What Hermes accesses

  • Google Calendar: to read, create and change events and send reminders to its owner.
  • Google Drive: to store and retrieve the owner's files.
  • Google Photos: to upload photos and read only the photos Hermes itself uploaded.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide these features to the owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Access tokens are stored on the owner's own computer.

Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
